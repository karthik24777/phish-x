Photo walk notes

Last Sunday we walked around the lake and took photos at sunrise. Here are a few tips for beginners: bring water, shoot early, and keep the horizon level. Thanks to everyone who came. The next walk is planned for the first weekend of next month, and the route will be shared on this page a few days before. If you are new, just turn up; bring any camera, a phone is fine.
